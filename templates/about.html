About NotesApp

Welcome to NotesApp! This simple web application lets you create, manage, and organize your personal notes online.

Features include:

  • User registration and secure login
  • Create, update, and delete notes
  • View all notes in a clean interface
  • Responsive design using Bootstrap

Our goal is to help you keep your ideas and tasks organized effortlessly.
